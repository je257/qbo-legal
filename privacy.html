QuickBooks Online Integration — Privacy Policy

Operator: HW (hwexec.com) Effective: July 20, 2026 Version 1.0

This policy describes how the QuickBooks Online integration operated by HW ("we," "us") accesses, uses, and protects the accounting data it reads from a connected QuickBooks Online company.

01 What this application is

This is a private software integration that connects a QuickBooks Online company to an AI assistant used internally by the account owner. It is not a public product and is not marketed to or made available to third parties. It is used solely by the individual or business that authorizes it against their own QuickBooks Online company.

This application is an independent integration built on Intuit's public developer API. It is not created, endorsed, or affiliated with Intuit Inc. "QuickBooks" and "QuickBooks Online" are trademarks of Intuit Inc.

02 Data we access

When you authorize the application, Intuit grants it access under the accounting scope. Depending on the actions you request, this may include:

  • Company profile information (name, address, fiscal year, currency);
  • Customers, vendors, employees, and items;
  • Transactions such as invoices, bills, payments, and journal entries;
  • Accounting reports (profit & loss, balance sheet, aging, and similar).

We access this data only in response to actions you initiate through your assistant, and only for the connected company.

03 How the data is used

Accessed data is used exclusively to fulfill the request you make — for example, retrieving a report, listing records, or creating and updating entries you direct. Data is returned to you through your own assistant. We do not sell, rent, or share your QuickBooks data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

04 Storage and security

Authentication is handled through Intuit's OAuth 2.0. The application stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection, kept locally on the operator's own machine with access-restricted permissions. QuickBooks records themselves are not retained in a separate database; they are fetched on demand and held only transiently to complete each request.

05 Data retention

Stored authentication tokens persist until you disconnect the application or they expire. We retain no long-term copy of your accounting data beyond what is momentarily processed to answer a request.

06 Revoking access

You may revoke the application's access at any time from within QuickBooks Online, under Settings → Apps (Manage connected apps), or by deleting the locally stored tokens. Revoking access immediately ends the application's ability to read or write your company data.

07 Children's privacy

This application is a business tool and is not directed to, or intended for use by, anyone under the age of 18.

08 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the integration evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a revised effective date and version above.
